A complete two-semester, calculus-based first-year university physics course. Every chapter builds the ideas step by step with diagrams, works examples in full between topics, and ends with graded practice problems: Level A concept checks, Level B standard problems and Level C challenge problems. Read each chapter online, or download its notes as a PDF. The full step-by-step solutions to its practice problems come as a separate PDF.

The same notes serve teachers and students. A teacher can lecture straight from the sections and examples. A student can read them alone, trying each "Think first" question before reading on.
